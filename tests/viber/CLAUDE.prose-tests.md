# tests/viber - the prose tests that bind shipped wording

Read for a change to a `*-prose.unit.test.ts` file, or to shipped markdown or script text one of them reads. The node holds the suite's other contracts.

## Contracts

- Twenty-seven `*-prose.unit.test.ts` files read shipped markdown and scripts as text, each holding the wording a flow depends on: `reach-prose` (the reach targets with the `| new` form, the `DECIDE:` rule and the split limits of `plan-surveyor`, the outline step of `planner` with the header of `plan-build.ts` it runs, the reach file as `plan-architect` and the four plan reviewers read it, the `## Reach` of a slice brief), `planner-agents-prose` (the carrier rule of the four planning agents and the four plan reviewers: no `Bash`, `plan:` first, the `out:` line and the `OUT:` reply; the `PENDING` skeleton across `plan-architect`, `plan-detailer`, `plan-fixer`, the spec and graph reviewers and the header of `plan-build.ts`; the `(inferred)`, `(untouched)` and `(behaviour)` markers across the reviewers, `plan-fixer` and `review-round.sh`; each reviewer's `## Input` against the member block of the `review-round.sh` header; every `viber:<name>` of the planner skill, `adr-tasks.md`, `plan-build` and `review-round.sh` listed in `plugin.json`), `lessons-prose` (the `--lessons` call and `run-lessons:` line of the close's fix and repair coders and of no final reviewer, the rule wording, the guides), `fixer-prose` (the tracing checks, the instrumentation exception and its `tree-record.sh` record and handoff gate in `fixer`, the `Same defect elsewhere` field and its place question, the matching exception of `manifest.md`), `e2e-prose` (the existing end-to-end spec exception across `plan-rules.md`, `test-strategy.md`, the `## Write` of `plan-architect.md` and the two Grounded checks), `final-review-prose` (the `--final-review` call, the `scope:` line of `final-reviewer`, and its and the fix coder's `memory:` line keeping every `CLAUDE.md` out of the final review), `phases-prose` (the continuation sentence and the next-phase line of both `implementor` close forms, read from `parts/close.md`, and the `## Phases` section of both spec templates), `previous-review-prose` (`implementor` building no `previous:` line, the re-review rule of `task-reviewer`), `close-part-prose` (the body pointer, the `allowed-tools` entries, the step 3 close entries, the step 7 token and trail calls and summary lines, the `attempts:` and `arbiter:` lines, steps 5 to 7 absent from `SKILL.md`, the `switch-fragments.md` wording, the `SAY=` bullets of `qa.true.md`), `nudge-prose` (no skill composes the nudge, each keeps its fallback), `guard-prose` (the `viber guard:` marker sentence and one-line guarded rules in the agents, the `cd` rule among them, the coder's interpreter line, the `Provable` plan rule, the marker in the guards' deny text, the `REFUSAL:` line of every `DENIED` output and of the skills' questions), `extras-prose` (the extras and `scope` lines and the `SCRATCH:` prose of `implementor`, `task-coder`, `task-reviewer`) and `ruling-prose` (the `ruling` line, every ruling reported through the `ruled` call, the resume directive of the stalled coder, in `implementor`, `task-coder` and `arbiter`), both joining `SKILL.md` and `parts/close.md` for their whole-file checks, `branching-stay-prose` (the `Branch:` bullet of the four `branching-handoff` fragments never names "stay"), `branching-prose` (the eight branching fragments run an option's `run:` and settle its `settle:`, holding no per-answer rule, and the `--settled` re-run sits in fixer's `branching-start` and intent's `branching-handoff` fragments, never in intent's `branching-start`), `e2e-steps-prose` (steps 4, 5 and 7 of `e2e` run `app-wait.sh`
