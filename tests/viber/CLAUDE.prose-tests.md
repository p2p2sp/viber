# tests/viber - the prose tests that bind shipped wording

Read for a change to a `*-prose.unit.test.ts` file, or to shipped markdown or script text one of them reads. The node holds the suite's other contracts.

## Contracts

- Twenty-six `*-prose.unit.test.ts` files read shipped markdown and scripts as text, each holding the wording a flow depends on: `impact-mapper-prose` (the `| new` target, implementers and constructors, the `implementation` and `collection` kinds across `impact-mapper` and the reach step of `planner`), `lessons-prose` (the `--lessons` call and `run-lessons:` line of the close's fix and repair coders and of no final reviewer, the rule wording, the guides), `fixer-prose` (the tracing checks, the instrumentation exception and its `tree-record.sh` record and handoff gate in `fixer`, the `Same defect elsewhere` field and its place question, the matching exception of `manifest.md`), `e2e-prose` (the existing end-to-end spec exception across `plan-rules.md`, `test-strategy.md`, `planner/SKILL.md` and the two Grounded checks), `final-review-prose` (the `--final-review` call, the `scope:` line of `final-reviewer`, and its and the fix coder's `memory:` line keeping every `CLAUDE.md` out of the final review), `roadmap-prose` (the continuation sentence and the next-part line of both `implementor` close forms, read from `parts/close.md`), `previous-review-prose` (`implementor` building no `previous:` line, the re-review rule of `task-reviewer`), `close-part-prose` (the body pointer, the `allowed-tools` entries, the step 3 close entries, the step 7 token and trail calls and summary lines, the `attempts:` and `arbiter:` lines, steps 5 to 7 absent from `SKILL.md`, the `switch-fragments.md` wording, the `SAY=` bullets of `qa.true.md`), `nudge-prose` (no skill composes the nudge, each keeps its fallback), `guard-prose` (the `viber guard:` marker sentence and one-line guarded rules in the agents, the coder's interpreter line, the `Provable` plan rule, the marker in the guards' deny text), `extras-prose` (the extras and `scope` lines and the `SCRATCH:` prose of `implementor`, `task-coder`, `task-reviewer`) and `ruling-prose` (the `ruling` line, every ruling reported through the `ruled` call, the resume directive of the stalled coder, in `implementor`, `task-coder` and `arbiter`), both joining `SKILL.md` and `parts/close.md` for their whole-file checks, `branching-stay-prose` (the `Branch:` bullet of the four `branching-handoff` fragments never names "stay"), `branching-prose` (the eight branching fragments run an option's `run:` and settle its `settle:`, holding no per-answer rule, and the `--settled` re-run sits in fixer's `branching-start` and intent's `branching-handoff` fragments, never in intent's `branching-start`), `e2e-steps-prose` (steps 4, 5 and 7 of `e2e` run `app-wait.sh` and `qa-ledger.sh`), `say-prose` (the fixer, intent and `issue-create` skip texts state `SAY=`, holding no reason list), `intent-prose` (the capped round with its `--questions` preload, the `Explore` wording, the closed signal list, the split proposal pointer and its rules in `references/split.md`, the `done` part with its summary file hand-off and plan mode exception, no fast path or spec shape proposal, and `START_TEXT_WORD_CAP` over `SKILL.md` plus `issues-input.false.md`, raised only on purpose), `root-cause-prose` (the optional `## Root cause` of both spec templates, its `plan-rules.md` rule and `memory-writer` writing it first as traps), `repair-prose` (the `repair` line of `task-coder` and `task-reviewer`, the `Repair pass:` paragraph of `implementor`, `report:` with no `reason:` after a review failure, `closeout`'s `LEFT:` line), `failure-trail-prose` (the `Blocking [<cause>]` finding line and the four cause labels, worded alike in both reviewers), `rules-trail-prose` (the trail direction across `rules-auditor`, `rule-admission.md`, `rules-writer` and the `rules` skill), `rules-close-prose` (`rules-writer` under the spec shape never adds, compacts or splits and reports `OVER:`, the `rule-admission.md` cap and the `/viber:rules` pointer of `rules.true.md`), `red-evidence-prose` (the `RED:` and `red-check:` lines across `task-coder`, `task-reviewer`, `implementor` and `parts/close.md`), `resume-prose` (the `dirty:` and `orphan:` bullets of `implementor`), `plan-review-prose` (every `(script)` rule in `plan-rules-script.md` with its error text, `plan-rules.md` keeping review rules only, the planner reviewers reading no scripted rule, the planner acting on the `FIX:`, `SPLIT:` and `review-round.sh` directives) and `test-pyramid-prose` (the test rules of `test-strategy.md`, with their byte caps, and the integration-test-written-never-run rule across `plan-rules.md`, `integration-tests.md`, `tdd.md`, `task-coder`, `task-reviewer` and `test-runner`).
