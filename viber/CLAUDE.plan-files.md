# viber - the plan and run files that several parts copy

Read for a change to the plan's task block, its `## Open items` section or the `## Notes` of a task file, its frontmatter or attachments, `status.md`, the planner's input, the root cause section or the failure trail: the files each of them touches. The node holds the other contracts of the pipeline.

## Change together

- The plan's task block (`<!-- TASK -->` ... `<!-- /TASK -->`, `### T<n> - <title>`, the `- <Field>:` lines): `skills/planner/templates/tasks.md`, `references/plan-rules.md`, `references/plan-rules-script.md`, `agents/plan-author.md` (writes every line, `- Depends-on: none` on each), `lib/plan-text.ts` and `lib/plan-build.ts` (order and number the tasks, read the task ids of `Delivers`, `Verification` and `DoD` through the new numbers, rename a slice's `L<n>` contract block to the next free `C<k>`), `lib/plan-index/` (`read.ts` and `check.ts` validate, `split.ts` splits), `lib/task-ready.ts` (reads `Depends-on`, `Exclusive` and `Files`), `lib/file-guard.ts` (reads the first `- Files:` line of each `tasks/<id>.md`) and `scripts/commit-task.sh` (commit subject from the heading, staging from `Files:`). A task's `Covers:` cites criteria as `AC<n>`, as do the QA documents of `references/qa-format.md`: `#<n>` would render as a link to an unrelated issue.
- The `- Depends-on:` line: no author writes a dependency. `orderTasks` of `lib/plan-graph.ts` computes it for `plan-build.sh assemble` and `apply`, once `arrangeTasks` put each task behind the producer of what it uses, from three rules (a shared `Files` path, a contract block one task writes and the other uses, a file of an earlier task named in `Verification`, `Delivers` or `DoD`), the same three `lib/plan-index/check.ts` holds a landed plan to and `references/plan-rules.md`'s `Ordered` tells an author to name; `skills/planner/templates/tasks.md`, `agents/plan-author.md` and `agents/plan-architect.md` write `none`.
- The plan's `## Open items` section (`- critical|note | <task id>|plan | <text>`, the last section above `## Tasks`, at most 3 notes per task): written only by `lib/plan-build.ts` through `withOpenItems` of `lib/plan-text.ts`, from the `CRITICAL:` and `NOTE:` lines of the judge file (`agents/plan-judge.md`) and the `fix:`, `unfixed:` and `GAP:` lines of a fix file (`agents/plan-author.md`, `agents/plan-architect.md`); parsed by `openItemsOf`; validated by `lib/plan-index/read.ts` and `check.ts`; shown to the user as the `OPEN:` lines of `NEXT: exit-plan` (`skills/planner/SKILL.md` step 5); listed by `lib/run-notes.ts`. For a plan written outside the planner the session writes the section itself, told by `hooks/content/plan-hints.md` and the deny text of `hooks/scripts/plan-gate.sh`.
- The `## Notes` of a task file (`- N<k>: <text>`, numbered from 1 in section order, only on a task with a `note | T<n>` item): `lib/plan-index/split.ts` writes it and cuts `## Open items` out of `spec.md`; `agents/task-coder.md` answers each with an `N<k>:` line at the top of its notes file, `agents/task-reviewer.md` and `agents/final-reviewer.md` read both, and `lib/run-notes.ts` numbers the notes the same way to pair each with its coder line.
- The plan's `attachments:` map and the `attachments/<name>` form a task's `DoD` names: `skills/planner/templates/spec-full.md`/`spec-lite.md`, `agents/plan-architect.md` and, writing a whole plan, `agents/plan-author.md` (fill the map from the input's `Attachment:` lines), `agents/plan-author.md` (names an entry in a task's `DoD`), `scripts/plan-path.sh` (validates, lands), `scripts/plan-index.sh` (validates, lists in `spec.md`), `agents/task-coder.md` and `agents/task-reviewer.md`.
- `status.md`'s `done:`/`skipped:` lines: written by `commit-task.sh`, read by `plan-index.sh`, `lib/task-ready.ts`, `lib/run-notes.ts` and `lib/file-guard.ts`.
- `status.md`'s `refused: <task id>:<path> ...` key, present only once a task commit refused a path another open task claims: written by `commit-task.sh` in that commit (a space, a tab or a `%` of a path as `%20`, `%09` or `%25`, the key being split on blanks), read back by `lib/run-notes.ts` for the `- refused | ` lines of `outcome.md` and, through `scripts/run-summary.sh`, for the `refused:` lines of the final summary of every close.
- The `status.md` line `decision: <id>: sequenced after <other>: both need <path>`: `scripts/task-step.sh` prints its `commit-task.sh --decide` call as a `RUN:` line on the second collision of a pair, or on the first with a claimant that is not in flight, `lib/task-ready.ts` holds `<id>` back while `<other>` is neither done nor skipped, and `lib/run-notes.ts` lists it as a `- sequenced | ` line.
- The plan frontmatter `source:` line: `skills/planner/templates/spec-full.md`/`spec-lite.md`, the stub `skills/planner/SKILL.md` step 1 writes, `agents/plan-architect.md` and `agents/plan-author.md` (their `plan:` line), `lib/plan-build.ts` (`frame` and `assemble` refuse a plan text not opening on `---`, and with `apply` one whose frontmatter names no `source:`), `skills/implementor/SKILL.md` step 1 and `hooks/scripts/plan-gate.sh` (picks `plan-judge` over `outside-plan-review`); `scripts/plan-path.sh` adds the `planned:` line (the plan-mode path) that `scripts/run-summary.sh` reads to count the plan review rounds and planning tokens.
- The planner's input: `skills/intent/parts/done.md` writes the confirmed summary to the path `skills/intent/scripts/summary-path.sh` prints (`.temp/viber/intent/summary-<stamp>.md`) and passes it (text inside plan mode, `fixer` always text); `skills/planner/SKILL.md`, `skills/planner/scripts/plan-build.sh` (`survey --input` keeps the path and prints it as the `input:` line of its blocks, `add: input` without one), `agents/plan-surveyor.md`, `agents/plan-architect.md`, `agents/plan-author.md` (writing a whole plan or fixing one with no brief; a slice author takes none), `agents/plan-judge.md`, `skills/planner/references/adr-tasks.md` and `agents/adr-screener.md` each take `input:` as that path or text.
- The root cause of a fixed defect: the optional `## Root cause` of `skills/planner/templates/spec-full.md`/`spec-lite.md`, its `references/plan-rules.md` rule, `scripts/plan-index.sh --input` and `agents/memory-writer.md`.
- The failure trail's `- <cause> | <source> | <text>` entries: `scripts/run-failures.sh` (parses the finding bracket, adds the cause `test`, prints the `failures:` line whose `convention` and `runs` counts `implementor`'s `parts/close.md` reads), the `Blocking [<cause>]` line of `agents/task-reviewer.md` and `agents/final-reviewer.md` (causes `convention`, `place`, `plan`, `task`; `task-reviewer` copies a carried-over finding's first line verbatim, so a repeat merges once), `skills/rules/scripts/rules-map.sh` (counts) and `agents/rules-auditor.md` (reads the `- convention | ` lines); `run-failures.sh --lessons` reads the same Blocking lines for the test-strategy `` `<Name>` `` spelling of `task-reviewer`.
