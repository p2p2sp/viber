# viber - the plan and run files that several parts copy

Read for a change to the plan's task block, its frontmatter or attachments, `status.md`, the planner's input, the root cause section or the failure trail: the files each of them touches. The node holds the other contracts of the pipeline.

## Change together

- The plan's task block (`<!-- TASK -->` ... `<!-- /TASK -->`, `### T<n> - <title>`, the `- <Field>:` lines): `skills/planner/templates/tasks.md`, `references/plan-rules.md`, `references/plan-rules-script.md`, `scripts/plan-index.sh` (validates, splits), and `scripts/commit-task.sh` (commit subject from the heading, staging from `Files:`). A task's `Covers:` cites criteria as `AC<n>`, as do the QA documents of `references/qa-format.md`: `#<n>` would render as a link to an unrelated issue.
- The plan's `attachments:` map and the `attachments/<name>` form a task's `DoD` names: `skills/planner/templates/spec-full.md`/`spec-lite.md`, `agents/plan-writer.md`, `agents/planner-review.md`, `agents/planner-spec-review.md`, `scripts/plan-path.sh` (validates, lands), `scripts/plan-index.sh` (validates, lists in `spec.md`), `agents/task-coder.md` and `agents/task-reviewer.md`.
- `status.md`'s `done:`/`skipped:` lines: written by `commit-task.sh`, read by `plan-index.sh`.
- The plan frontmatter `source:` line: `skills/planner/templates/spec-full.md`/`spec-lite.md`, `agents/plan-writer.md` (its `plan:` line), `implementor/SKILL.md` step 1 and `hooks/scripts/plan-gate.sh` (picks the planner's reviewers over `outside-plan-review`); `scripts/plan-path.sh` adds the `planned:` line (the plan-mode path) that `scripts/run-summary.sh` reads to count the plan review rounds and planning tokens.
- The planner's input: `skills/intent/parts/done.md` writes the confirmed summary to the path `skills/intent/scripts/summary-path.sh` prints (`.temp/viber/intent/summary-<stamp>.md`) and passes it (text inside plan mode, `fixer` always text); `skills/planner/SKILL.md`, `agents/plan-writer.md`, `skills/planner/scripts/review-round.sh`, `references/adr-tasks.md`, `agents/planner-review.md`, `agents/planner-spec-review.md`, `agents/adr-screener.md` and the deny text of `hooks/scripts/plan-gate.sh` each take `input:` as that path or text.
- The root cause of a fixed defect: the optional `## Root cause` of `skills/planner/templates/spec-full.md`/`spec-lite.md`, its `references/plan-rules.md` rule, `scripts/plan-index.sh --input` and `agents/memory-writer.md`.
- The failure trail's `- <cause> | <source> | <text>` entries: `scripts/run-failures.sh` (parses the finding bracket, adds the cause `test`, prints the `failures:` line whose `convention` and `runs` counts `implementor`'s `parts/close.md` reads), the `Blocking [<cause>]` line of `agents/task-reviewer.md` and `agents/final-reviewer.md` (causes `convention`, `place`, `plan`, `task`; `task-reviewer` copies a carried-over finding's first line verbatim, so a repeat merges once), `skills/rules/scripts/rules-map.sh` (counts) and `agents/rules-auditor.md` (reads the `- convention | ` lines); `run-failures.sh --lessons` reads the same Blocking lines for the test-strategy `` `<Name>` `` spelling of `task-reviewer`.
